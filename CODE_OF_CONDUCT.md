# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We are committed to a respectful, harassment-free community for everyone
participating in this project, regardless of age, body size, disability,
ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of
experience, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, personal
appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our Standards

Positive behavior includes:

- Using welcoming and inclusive language.
- Respecting differing viewpoints and experiences.
- Accepting constructive feedback gracefully.
- Prioritizing the health of the community.
- Showing empathy toward other contributors.

Unacceptable behavior includes:

- Sexualized language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention or advances.
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks.
- Public or private harassment.
- Publishing another person's private information without explicit permission.
- Other conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Maintainer Responsibilities

Maintainers are responsible for clarifying expected standards and taking fair,
appropriate action in response to unacceptable behavior. They may remove,
edit, or reject comments, commits, code, issues, and other contributions that
do not align with this Code of Conduct, and may temporarily or permanently
restrict participation for harmful behavior.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in all project spaces and in public spaces when
someone is representing the project.

## Enforcement

Report abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior privately to
`security@openclaw.ai` with `Babelfish conduct report` in the subject. Reports
will be reviewed confidentially to the extent possible and handled
proportionally to the circumstances.
